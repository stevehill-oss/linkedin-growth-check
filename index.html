          Straight answers

          How much of my time will it take?

          More at the start, much less after. The first conversation needs a proper block of your time, because everything I write comes from it. After that it’s mostly reading drafts and approving them, plus a short catch-up when something’s worth writing about.

          Will it sound like me?

          That’s the job. I work from how you actually talk: your opinions, your stories, the phrases you use with clients. Expect more back and forth in the first few weeks while I learn how you like things said. If a draft doesn’t sound like you, send it back.

          Do I approve everything before it goes out?

          Yes. Every post comes to you as a draft. Edit it, send it back or approve it. If you don’t like a post, it doesn’t go out, and nothing is published in your name without your yes.

          Is what I tell you kept confidential?

          Yes. What you tell me in our conversations stays between us. I don’t name clients or share anything about our work together unless you’ve agreed to it in writing.

          Do you need my LinkedIn password?

          No, never. For your personal profile, I write drafts, you approve them, and either you post them or a scheduling tool you’ve connected yourself does. For a company page, you add me as an admin in LinkedIn’s own settings and can remove me whenever you like.
